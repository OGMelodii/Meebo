## Introducing the "What Did You Expect?" Bot or The "MEE6" Destroyer

Tired of banning, mute, and kicking people in Discord like it's a full-time job?

Does it feel like you're stuck being some sort of digital babysitter, sorting through the chaotic mess while chronically online folks debate the merits of pineapple on pizza, as if that debate hadn't been beaten to death by scores of memes?

This bot will free you from the existential dread of moderating an online community and into more important things-just like judging other people's choices while your own life falls apart, like a stale cookie under the weight of bad decisions.

## What This Bot Can Do

But behold, here comes the bot with a dazzling array of commands that might even make the most power-mad of Discord mods weep into their Cheez-Its.

- **!ban @user**: Ban a user. Sometimes, all they bring to the table is a stream of bad puns relentless enough to make a dad joke enthusiast cry. Nobody needs that negativity in their life. You aren't running a comedy club, so why put up with amateur hour? You deserve better!

- **!unban @user**: Unbans a user by mention. When one suddenly feels like that guy isn't that insufferable. Or, better said, when one just wants to give them a second chance to screw up his day. Well, here goes chaos and don't say I didn't warn you.

- **!kick @user**: Kicks a user. (A gentle nudge out the door-more like a shove, really. It's not like they'll miss your server; they'll just find another place to annoy everyone!)

- **!mute @user**: Mutes a user indefinitely. (Silence is golden, and this user is now on permanent mute until you decide to unmute them. Enjoy the sweet sound of nothingness!)

- **!unmute @user**: Unmutes a user. (For those brave enough to let them speak again—proceed with caution. Remember, you’ve muted them for a reason!)

- **!warn @user**: Warns and mutes a user for 5 minutes. (A gentle reminder for the most annoying of the bunch. “Congratulations! You’ve unlocked ‘Most Likely to Ruin a Server.’”)

- **!serverinfo**: Displays information about the server. Spoiler alert: It's probably just a bunch of people debating the merits of cats vs. dogs while living in their parents' basements.

- **!info**: Lists all available commands. Perfect for the illiterate.

- **!userinfo @user**: Displays information about a user. For when you need to remember who's been a pain.

- **!muteall**: Mute all members. Finally, some peace and quiet! Enjoy the sound of crickets.

- **!unmuteall**: Unmute all members. Brace yourself—chaos is coming!

- **!promote @user (role)**: Promotes a user to a specified role. Good luck explaining that to those who thought they had power.

- **!demote @user (role)**: Demotes a user from a specified role. Because they were never fit to wear that crown, anyway.

- **!chanpro @user (CHANNELID)**: Sets a user as channel producer. For those who might actually know what they're doing—don't get your hopes up.

- **!depro @user (CHANNELID)**: Removes a user as channel producer. No hard feelings… until the next server meeting.

- **!setowner [user_id]**: Changes the OWNER_ID to the specified user. Because sometimes you just need to hand over the keys.

- **!getowner**: Shows the current OWNER_ID. For when you forget who's in charge.

- **!resetowner**: Resets the OWNER_ID to the server owner. Because sometimes it's best to go back to square one.

- **!slowdown (DURATION) (CHANNEL_ID)**: Slows down sending messages in the given channel. For when the chaos needs to be reigned in, if only temporarily.

## Installation

Setting up this bot is easier than trying to ignore Discord moderators begging to do rule enforcement on nobody-cares rules.

1. Download the repository for the bot files-you should know how to do that, right? If you don't, take a minute to reconsider life choices.
2. Go outside and touch grass. Seriously, it's fine, it will be here when you return.
3. Run the setup script, and watch as it magically installs, provided you actually remember how to run scripts and don't accidentally delete your entire server in the process.
4. Take a minute to reflect on life choices. This is entirely optional but highly advised.
5. Configure settings to your liking. This takes a bit of thought to pull off, so good luck with that-no pressure!
6. Get a girlfriend or boyfriend (we don't judge). Because nothing says "I'm ready for chaos" like having someone to share the madness with!
7. Buy a stress ball or something equally squishy, just in case things get out of hand. You might need it when the drama hits the fan!
8. Add the bot to your server and watch as it turns your server into a semi-organized chaos arena!
